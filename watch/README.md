# Watch Component

Reserved for approved Amazfit Bip 6 / Zepp OS product source. Product
implementation is blocked until FEA-001 and Gate G0 confirm supported APIs,
sensor access, background execution, storage, communication, power, and
deployment limits.

Non-product feasibility experiments are isolated under `feasibility/`. They may
prove toolchain or device compatibility but do not establish medical fitness,
sensor suitability, or authorization for product implementation.
